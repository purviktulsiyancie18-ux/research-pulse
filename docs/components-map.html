Kavita at the edges

  • She sets the domains and decides what is useful, what to bookmark and what to bring to a client
  • AI only filters, summarises and ranks in the middle

Guardrails

  • Only papers from the date window; no paper is sent twice
  • Every ranking shows why; Gemini outage still sends titles + abstracts
  • Nothing goes to clients; the brief reaches Kavita only

Out of scope (extend later)

  • OpenAlex for journals beyond arXiv · GitHub stars as a signal
  • Extra recipients per domain · "Ask about this paper" Q&A
